## Statement
Find number of 2 disjoint sets of $\{2,\dots,n\}$ $S$ and $T$, such that $\forall i,j: \gcd(S_i,T_j)=1$.

## Step 1

We can record the set of prime factors of each set. That gives $f[i,p_S,p_T]$. The number of states explode to $O(n 4^{\frac{n}{\ln n}})$.

## Step 2

For any number in $[2, n]$, there is at most $1$ prime factor larger than $\sqrt{n}$. We can only record the smaller prime factors.
