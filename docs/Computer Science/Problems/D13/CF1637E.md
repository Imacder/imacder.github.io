## Step 1

Given $f[i]$, and a set of unordered forbidden pairs $(u,v)$. Find $\max_{(i,j) \notin (u,v)}((f[i] + f[j]) (i + j))$.

## Step 2

$$
\begin{align}
(f[i] + f[j])(i + j) &= i \cdot f[i] + j \cdot f[j] + i \cdot f[j] + j \cdot f[i]
\end{align}
$$
The second part is really annoying. Is there something that allows a clean separation?

Observation: $\sum{f[i]} = n$, therefore there are only $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{n})$ kinds of $f[i]$.
