## Step 1

For every $i$, there is a set of items it can be exchanged for in $p$ and $q$. These form a contiguous prefix. Therefore we are trying to find the minimum contiguous prefix that contains $i$.

## Step 2

By minimum, I mean $p^{-1}$ and $q^{-1}$ contains a contiguous prefix permutation of $(1 \dots m)$ such that $m \ge i$ and is the smallest. Then we have the answer:
$$\sum{m_i (m_i - m_{i-1})}$$

## Step 3

We can use a segment tree for maintaining the answer. We can rephrase $m$ to be:
$$m = \min_{1 \le i \le m}{t_i}$$
where $t$ is the relative order of $p$ and $q$.
